Two of these aren't style nits

Everything else on this page is prose craft — sentence shape, tone, repetition. These two are internal contradictions a reviewer will read as a threat-model bug, not a wording choice:

  • Implementation, line ~910 calls the MCP server itself “possibly untrusted” — the inverse of the project's own non-negotiable framing (servers are the protected asset, agents are the threat), and it contradicts this same section's “benign-server assumption” stated only 26 lines earlier. See the Implementation section below for the fix and why the field treats these as two genuinely distinct threat categories.
  • Implementation, lines 955–956 claims a verdict “cannot be steered by text an attacker writes,” while the two mechanisms named one sentence earlier are, by construction, driven by that same text. The claim is true in a narrower sense than it's stated.
