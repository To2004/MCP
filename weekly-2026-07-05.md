## 💡 Prompting Tips
### **Context engineering now beats clever phrasing**
As models get stronger, the structure around a task matters more than wording. Structure everything Claude receives — system prompt, files, examples, role framing, constraints — and use a contract-style system prompt that defines the role and what "done" means, with source material wrapped in XML tags and the output schema spelled out.
**Do this now:** Convert one flaky prompt into a contract: define the role, wrap inputs in `<context>` tags, add 1–3 examples, and state the exact output format.
[Source](https://www.the-ai-corner.com/p/claude-best-practices-power-user-guide-2026)

### **Newer Claude takes you literally — be explicit**
The 4.5-generation models prioritize precise instructions over "helpful" guessing; ask for a dashboard and you may get a titled blank frame because you didn't spec the rest. Giving Claude explicit permission to express uncertainty also cuts hallucinations.
**Do this now:** Audit your top prompt for anything you're assuming Claude will "just know," and spell it out — including edge cases and what to do when unsure.
[Source](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/claude-prompting-best-practices)
