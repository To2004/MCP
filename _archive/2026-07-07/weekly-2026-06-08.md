## 💡 Prompting Tips
### **Stop using ALL-CAPS and "YOU MUST"**
Recent testing shows ALL-CAPS, "YOU MUST", and "NEVER EVER" overtrigger Claude and produce *worse* outputs. Reasoning also starts degrading past ~3,000 tokens; the sweet spot is 150–300 words.
**Do this now:** Audit your top system prompt — strip emphasis shouting and trim filler under 300 words. [Source](https://chatpromptgenius.com/claude-ai-prompt-engineering-guide-advanced-2026-techniques/)

### **Front-load and back-load critical context**
Long-context "lost in the middle" still bites Claude. Put your most important instructions and context in the first ~10% and last ~10% of the prompt where attention weights are highest.
**Do this now:** Restructure one long prompt today: critical instructions at top, restate constraints at bottom, supporting material in the middle. [Source](https://www.the-ai-corner.com/p/context-engineering-guide-2026)

### **Use Opus 4.7+ literally — drop negative instructions**
Opus 4.7 interprets prompts more literally than 4.6, especially at lower effort. Negative instructions ("don't do X") only stick to the literal sentence and leak. Rewrite as positive directives.
**Do this now:** Replace "don't include code in the answer" with "respond in prose only." [Source](https://miraflow.ai/blog/claude-opus-4-7-prompting-best-practices-2026)
