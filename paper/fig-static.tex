\caption{Static (design-time) scoring. Two documents---the server's advertised
tool catalog and the organization's written policy---feed three scanners, each
producing exactly one primitive: tool impact $i_t$ over the $T$ tools, asset
sensitivity $s_a$ over the $A$ assets, and blast radius $b_{t,a}$ over all
$T\!\times\!A$ pairs. Their product is the severity matrix. Since $i$ and $s$ are
each indexed by a single dimension, $b$ is what distinguishes a cell from others
in its row; $b_{t,a}=\varnothing$ marks a tool that cannot reach an asset and
removes the cell. The organization states no severity number: $s_a$ is derived by
classifying each asset against the policy's own classes.}
